FAQ

Is BetterAudio compatible with my Mac?

Yes, it works on macOS Ventura and later, optimized for Apple Silicon and Intel.

How do I install the CLI?

Open BetterAudio preferences, navigate to the General tab, and follow the installation prompt in the last section.

Can I hide specific devices?

Absolutely—enable the option to hide Bluetooth devices or others in preferences.

What’s the adaptive menu bar icon?

It dynamically changes to represent your currently active audio device.
